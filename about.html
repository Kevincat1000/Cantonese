About Me & This Project

Hi, I’m Kevin. This project started in a pretty simple way. I really like dim sum. Growing up, it was something I looked forward to. Over time, I started wondering how Cantonese traditions ended up so far from where they began.

Last summer, I had the chance to go to Hong Kong, and that experience made everything feel more real. It pushed me to look beyond just enjoying the culture and start asking questions about where it came from and how it travels.

I like filming and recording things, so I decided to explore this through interviews and documentation instead of just reading about it. For this project, I’m talking to people from different generations about their families, the languages they speak, and the traditions they keep. The goal is to understand how Cantonese culture moves across places and time through the lens of ordinary routines and everyday stories.
