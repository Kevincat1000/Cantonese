The Linguistic Journey

Cantonese as Linguistic Capital

Beyond communication, language is capital. For over a century, Cantonese served as the primary currency of North American Chinatowns, granting access to employment, protection, and social belonging.

飲茶

(Yam Cha)

Drinking tea

Going for dim sum, eating, and socializing.

食檸檬

(Sik Ning Mung)

Eating lemons

Being rejected when asking someone out.

放飛機

(Fong Fei Gei)

Throwing an airplane

Breaking a promise to meet.

吹水

(Ceoi Seoi)

Blowing water

Chit-chatting; sometimes implies bragging.
