飲茶

(Yam Cha)

Drinking tea

Going for dim sum (often for brunch or lunch), eating the food, and socializing.

食檸檬

(Sik Ning Mung)

Eating lemons

Being rejected when asking someone out.

放飛機

(Fong Fei Gei)

Throwing an airplane

Breaking a promise to meet.

吹水

(Ceoi Seoi)

Blowing water

Chit-chatting; sometimes implies bragging or bluffing.

食咗飯未？

(Sik Jo Faan Mei?)

Have you eaten yet?

A casual greeting meaning “hello” or “how are you?”

生舊叉燒好過生你

(Sang Gau Char Siu Hou Gwo Sang Nei)

Better to give birth to char siu

“You’re frustrating me.” A classic parent complaint.

炒魷魚

(Caau Yau Yu)

Stir-fried squid

Getting fired.

雞同鴨講

(Gai Tung Aap Gong)

Chicken talking to a duck

Mutual lack of understanding; unable to communicate due to language barriers.
