1984–2000

The Hong Kong migration and suburban growth

“We brought our investments and our future.”
— Hong Kong migrant, late 1980s

Following the 1984 agreement that scheduled the return of Hong Kong to China for 1997, a massive migration wave occurred due to political uncertainty.

Unlike previous groups, these migrants were often wealthy professionals and investors who settled directly in suburban areas.

This wave shifted Cantonese migration from historic labor districts to globally connected suburbs.
