Faith: Temples, Ancestors, and Community Survival 信仰：庙宇、祖先与社群生存

For Cantonese migrants, faith was not only belief. It was a social system: rituals, mutual aid, lineage memory, and public culture that made “home” rebuildable in a foreign land. 对广东移民而言，信仰不只是“相信”。它是一套社会系统：仪式、互助、宗族记忆，以及让“家”得以重建的公共文化。
