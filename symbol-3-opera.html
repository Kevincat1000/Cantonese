Symbol 3: Cantonese Opera

Theme: The Stage as a Repository of Transnational Memory

Cantonese Opera serves as a profound medium for cultural memory, manifesting Lingnan history through the "stage" in overseas diasporas. Historically, it functioned not just as entertainment, but as a ritualistic "Chou-shen" (thanksgiving to deities) performance that bonded immigrant communities. Its significance lies in its dual role: as a carrier of the Cantonese language and as a rigorous system of physical aesthetics. The opera’s tradition of acrobatics and stylized combat provided the foundational "body knowledge" for later action cinema. In the transnational context, Cantonese Opera became a mobile "homeland," allowing immigrants to relive collective narratives and maintain their ethnic identity through the sight, sound, and symbolism of the traditional stage.
